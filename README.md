# powerhive_V1
PowerHive – Ideation Phase Presentation (Body Section)

Problem Statement • Many households face electrical hazards due to poor knowledge, faulty wiring, and lack of monitoring. • Rising incidents of home electrical fires and shocks show the need for a safer, smarter system. • Homeowners lack visibility into their electrical ecosystem.

Proposed Solution: PowerHive • A smart monitoring system for household electrical installations. • Uses sensors, embedded systems, and real-time alerts. • Helps users detect faults early and understand their electricity usage.

Key Features • Real-time monitoring of voltage, current, and temperature. • Automated fault detection (overloads, overheating, short circuits). • Mobile or web dashboard for user interaction. • Alerts sent instantly when danger is detected. • Educational tips for electrical safety.

Impact • Reduces preventable electrical accidents. • Increases household safety and awareness. • Aligns with Rwanda’s NST2 goals (smart infrastructure and ICT development). • Supports SDG 7 (Affordable and Clean Energy) and SDG 9 (Industry, Innovation, Infrastructure).

Target Users • Households • Small businesses • Electricians and technicians • Future extension: HEP stations for advanced monitoring

Project Feasibility • Uses affordable hardware (sensors, Arduino/ESP32). • Can be tested on a small prototype. • Scalable in future versions.

Conclusion • PowerHive is a practical and impactful solution. • It strengthens electrical safety and empowers users with real-time awareness.
